### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at FCJ is very friendly, open, and always emphasizes mutual support. Program members are always ready to help when I encounter difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace is organized neatly and comfortably, helping me maintain focus and high work efficiency. However, I think the program could organize more activities or events to strengthen connections between members and exchange new knowledge.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
Mentors have provided me with very dedicated support with clear and easy-to-understand guidance. The admin team also provided excellent support regarding procedures and documentation to ensure a smooth internship process.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The internship content and AWS project were highly relevant to my Information Technology major. I had the opportunity to apply theory to practice by deploying web applications on a cloud platform with services such as EC2, VPC, RDS, S3, and IAM, significantly bridging the gap between academic knowledge and business practice.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The internship provided me with many opportunities to improve both my technical skills and problem-solving abilities. Experiences in system deployment and optimization helped me better understand how systems operate in practice. More importantly, I realized the importance of continuous learning to adapt to new technologies.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The work culture here is positive and highly professional. Everyone respects each other and maintains a work environment that is both serious and supportive.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company has provided flexible learning schedules. In addition, participating in events has helped me quickly improve my practical knowledge.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **For the University and the Faculty of Information Technology:** I suggest that the Faculty integrate more courses on cloud computing (especially AWS) into the official curriculum. Simultaneously, increasing practical sessions (labs) and seminars with businesses will help students feel less overwhelmed when entering the real-world environment.

- **For the company/program:** I hope the program continues to maintain and develop networking activities to create a dynamic learning and exchange environment among interns.
